The Metaphor: Nuts & Eggs

A sensible, intuitive mental model for personal knowledge management.

🌰

The Nut: Raw Captures

Whenever you find an interesting article, tweet thread, or YouTube video, NutEgg extracts its raw content into a "nut". Complete with metadata, timestamps, and full context stored safely in nutegg/_raw/.

🥚

The Egg: Structured Knowledge

An "egg" is a living, topic-specific knowledge tree in your Obsidian vault. Each egg contains defined scopes, key questions, and an organized hierarchy of concepts you actually care about.

🐣

The Hatch: Novelty Synthesis

AI compares incoming nuts against your existing eggs. Instead of dumping duplicate notes into your vault, NutEgg isolates the novel deltas and weaves them directly into your knowledge structure.

How It Works

From browsing the open web to curated Obsidian knowledge in seconds.

1

Open NutEgg on Any Webpage

Click the extension icon to summon the side panel. NutEgg automatically grabs the article text, X thread, or YouTube transcript for the active tab.

2

Instant Anti-Clickbait & Summary

Get a direct 1-sentence answer to the title's hook, a 3-sentence executive summary, and an explicit "Should You Read It?" verdict.

3

AI Evaluates Against Your Existing Vault

Your local Obsidian plugin matches the content against your _index.md eggs. It highlights truly new ideas while filtering out what you already know.

4

Hatch or Collect

Click 🥚 Hatch Egg to weave insights directly into your matching knowledge tree, or 🌰 Collect Nut to archive the raw content for future reference.

What You Should Not Expect

Honest boundaries on where NutEgg excels and what it is not designed for.

  • No magic buttons: You won't get smarter just by clicking a button. Truly mastering knowledge still requires active reading, critical thinking, and deliberate practice.
  • Built for fluff & clickbait, not deep textbooks: NutEgg shines at saving time on bloated videos, repetitive posts, and AI-inflated articles. It is not intended for processing comprehensive textbooks or full online courses into instant expertise.
  • Not tailored for entertainment: NutEgg is built for density and insight extraction, not for summarizing comedy, films, or entertainment videos.
  • Work in progress: Support for podcasts, PDF files, and additional specialized extractors is actively under development.
