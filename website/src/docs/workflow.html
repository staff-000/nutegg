AI Workflow & Prompt Engine

Learn how NutEgg's modular workflow engine orchestrates content extraction, summary-based egg routing, novelty comparison, and user-customizable prompts.

The Two-Stage Architecture

Rather than running a single monolithic prompt, NutEgg uses a Two-Stage Architecture designed for token efficiency, high extraction precision, and complete user control:

💡 Why Summary-Based Routing?

Routing eggs using the Stage 1 summary instead of full raw articles or multi-hour video transcripts saves tens of thousands of tokens per run and improves routing accuracy by focusing on distilled, high-signal semantic themes.

Execution Modes

Mode Behavior Best Used For
📱 Standalone Mode Runs Stage 1 content analysis directly in Chrome (when Obsidian is offline or closed). Produces verdicts, 3-sentence summaries, chapter maps, mind maps, and interactive video Q&A with timestamp jumping. Skips Stage 2 vault comparison. Fast web and video reading, quick comprehension, or when you don't need note archiving.
⚡ Fast Mode Runs Stage 1 content analysis, routes eggs automatically via _index.md, and immediately executes Stage 2 knowledge comparison in one uninterrupted pass. Everyday reading and quick captures when you trust automatic egg matching.
🥚 Confirm Eggs Mode Runs Stage 1 content analysis, then pauses in the popup. Shows matched eggs alongside your vault's full egg list. You can add/remove eggs, proceed with knowledge comparison, or click Collect Nut Only. Deep research, ambiguous topics, or when you only want a quick summary without updating egg knowledge trees.

Workflow Files in Your Vault

NutEgg seeds its prompt templates into nutegg/_workflow/ inside your vault:

1. Shared Fragments (Injected into other prompts)

  • shared-output-rules.md: Grounding directive (source content as sole truth) and multi-lingual output language reference rules. Injected as {{shared_output_rules}}.
  • content-task-default.md: Core task instructions for title verdicts, 3-bullet summaries, and chapter maps. Injected as {{content_task_default}}.

2. Content Capture & Comparison Pipeline

  • content-analysis.md: Stage 1 content summary, anti-clickbait verdict, interactive mind map, chapter map, and custom question answers with timestamp citations.
  • egg-routing.md: Matches Stage 1 summaries against egg descriptions in _index.md to select candidate eggs.
  • egg-analysis.md: Stage 2 per-egg extraction of candidate knowledge entries and key question answers scoped to one egg's instructions.
  • egg-compare.md: Stage 2 knowledge diff against the existing # Knowledge tree to identify true novel insights and deliver a read verdict.
  • aggregate-content.md & aggregate-egg.md: Long content (>30k characters) chunk synthesizers for Stage 1 and Stage 2.

3. Interactive & Maintenance Workflows

  • follow-up.md: Interactive natural-language Q&A in the side panel with prior conversation history and clickable video timestamp jumping.
  • merge-unprocessed.md: Triggered manually or automatically at 20+ pending entries to organize and nest raw items into # Knowledge.
  • localize-egg.md: Translates new egg templates into foreign languages while preserving English structural headings.

Safe Customization Guidelines

⚠️ What to Preserve

You are free to customize prompt tone, perspective, and depth. However, you must preserve:

  • All {{placeholder}} tags (e.g. {{content}}, {{knowledge_tree}}, {{title}}).
  • Exact JSON output keys (e.g. titleVerdict, novelDelta, mindMap) so the parser can deserialize the response.
  • Structural headings (# Knowledge, # Unprocessed) in markdown outputs.

Conflict-Free Updates & Reset

When NutEgg updates to a newer version:

  • Unedited Prompts: Automatically updated to the latest built-in version.
  • Customized Prompts: Your edits are never overwritten. NutEgg writes [filename].new.md alongside your file so you can review new additions at your own pace.
  • Factory Reset ("Use Defaults"): Need a fresh start? In Obsidian Settings → NutEgg, click Use Defaults. All existing prompts are safely backed up to nutegg/_workflow/_backup/<timestamp>/ and pristine factory defaults are restored.
