Analysis Workflow

⚡ Fast Mode: NutEgg summarizes page content and chapter map, automatically matches relevant eggs from your vault, and immediately proceeds to knowledge comparison so you get your reading verdict without extra clicks.

🎯 Confirm Eggs Mode: NutEgg summarizes the page and identifies relevant eggs, then pauses so you can review matched eggs, select or uncheck topics, or skip comparison before proceeding.

Content Analysis Sections

Configure which sections are generated during Content Analysis. Disabling unused sections completely removes them from the AI prompt and output schema, saving tokens and cutting latency.

Obsidian Plugin Companion

NutEgg is a two-part system: this Chrome Extension captures content from your browser, while the Obsidian plugin handles AI analysis and knowledge tree curation in your vault.
