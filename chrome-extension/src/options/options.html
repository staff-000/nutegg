Analysis Workflow

⚡ Fast Mode: NutEgg summarizes page content and chapter map, automatically matches relevant eggs from your vault, and immediately proceeds to knowledge comparison so you get your reading verdict without extra clicks.

🎯 Confirm Eggs Mode: NutEgg summarizes the page and identifies relevant eggs, then pauses so you can review matched eggs, select or uncheck topics, or skip comparison before proceeding.
